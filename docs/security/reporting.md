# Reporting a vulnerability

How to tell the Launchway maintainers about a security problem. For anyone who finds one.

Please report security problems privately through GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/JK-Magic-Creations/launchway/security/advisories/new)
rather than in a public issue. We will acknowledge the report and keep you informed.

Helpful things to include:

- the commit or version you tested (`launchway version`);
- how Launchway was configured (with secrets removed);
- the steps to reproduce, and what you expected instead;
- the impact as you see it.

The project's security policy is in
[SECURITY.md](https://github.com/JK-Magic-Creations/launchway/blob/main/SECURITY.md). Known limits
of the current design are listed on the [trust model](trust-model.md) page; reports about them are
still welcome when you find a consequence the page does not describe.
