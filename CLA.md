This agreement is adapted from the Individual Contributor License Agreement ("ICLA") V2.2 of The
Apache Software Foundation (<https://www.apache.org/licenses/icla.pdf>). Only these things were
changed: the party names (JK Magic Creations in place of The Apache Software Foundation), the way
the agreement is made (see "How to agree" at the end), and the removal of the Foundation's
nonprofit and public benefit undertaking, which does not apply to JK Magic Creations. Everything
else is the template's wording.

## How to agree

In place of the template's signature block, you agree to this CLA by commenting on your pull
request with the text below, filling in your full name and the e-mail address you use for your
commits. Your comment is your signature. It applies to the Contribution in that pull request and
to all your later Contributions to Launchway, so you only need to do it once. If you contribute on
behalf of a legal entity, say so in the comment and give the entity's name; you confirm that you
are authorised to bind it.

```text
I have read the Launchway Individual Contributor License Agreement (CLA.md) and I agree to it
for my Contributions to Launchway, present and future.
Name: <your full name>
E-mail: <your e-mail address>
```

The maintainer records your name, e-mail address, the date and a link to your comment. Until an
automated CLA bot is set up, nothing else is needed from you.
